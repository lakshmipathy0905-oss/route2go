Delete your Route2Go account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Route2Go account and all associated data.

This action cannot be undone

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, saved vehicles, trips, expenses, and all personal data from our systems. This process is irreversible.

How to request deletion

You can request account deletion in two ways:

  1. In-app (recommended)
    Open Route2Go → Profile → Delete account. This is the fastest method and triggers immediate deletion.
  2. By email
    Send an email to route2go1@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Include "Delete my Route2Go account" in the subject line. We will process your request within 30 days as required by applicable law (DPDP Act 2023, IT Act 2000).

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and authentication data
  • All saved trips, routes, and itinerary data
  • Vehicle information
  • Expenses and budget history
  • Saved places and favorites
  • Any other personal data linked to your account

Retention and legal obligations

We will permanently delete your data within 30 days of receiving your deletion request, except where retention is required by applicable law (e.g., tax, fraud prevention, or regulatory obligations). In such cases, we will retain only the minimum data necessary for the required period and delete it as soon as the legal obligation ends.

After deletion, your data cannot be recovered. If you have backed up any data externally (e.g., shared trips via link), those copies may persist beyond our control.

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or data retention, contact us at route2go1@gmail.com.
