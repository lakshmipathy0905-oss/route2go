## 1. Who we are
Route2Go is a road-trip planning app. This policy explains what data we
collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

## 2. What we collect
- **Account data**: your email/phone and Firebase user ID when you create an
  account.
- **Trip planning data**: origin and destination, route options, vehicle
  details, budget and itinerary choices.
- **Location**: only when you choose a route start/end or begin a live trip.
  We do not collect background location outside an active live trip.
- **Usage data**: anonymised screen and feature usage for analytics. This is
  off by default and can be disabled in Settings.
- **Device identifiers**: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push
  notifications, only after you opt in.

## 3. How we use it
To plan routes and costs, show stays and places along your route, sync your
saved trips and expenses across devices, send notifications you asked for, and
improve the app.

## 4. What we do NOT do
We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising without
your consent. Route2Go shows partner stays and may earn an affiliate commission
when you book; that never changes the price you pay on the partner site.

## 5. Sharing with partners
- Firebase (Google) — authentication and push notifications.
- Supabase — database and serverless functions.
- Booking partners — only what you submit on their site; we log the booking
  click to track commission.

## 6. How long we keep it
We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your
account at any time in Settings; deletion removes your trips, vehicles,
expenses, notifications and profile.

## 7. Your rights
Access, correct, export, or delete your data. Email privacy@route2go.example
(placeholder) and we will respond within 30 days.

## 8. Security
All traffic is over HTTPS. Identity is verified with Firebase ID tokens, and
privileged operations run server-side with the service key.

## 9. Children
Route2Go is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect their data.

Material changes will be announced in-app, and this page updated.
