## 1. Who we are
Route2Go ("the App") is a road-trip planning application operated by **Together
(Lakshmipathy and Team)**, with its operational contact address at **JP Nagar,
Kothanuru Dinne, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560076, India**. Route2Go is operated as a
personal, non-company venture. Where this policy says "we", "us" or "our", it
means the operator identified above.

This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, the choices you
have, and the legal basis on which we rely. It applies to everyone who uses the
App, including guests who use it without an account.

## 2. What we collect
- **Account data**: your email address, display name (if you provide one), and
  the Firebase user ID issued when you create an account (via Google Sign-In or
  email/password).
- **Trip planning data**: origin and destination, route options, vehicle
  details (fuel type and mileage), budget and itinerary choices, and your
  saved trips.
- **Location**: precise location is used **only** when you actively choose a
  route start/end point, or begin a live trip (for on-route deviation checks).
  We do **not** collect background location outside an active live trip, and we
  never upload a continuous GPS track to our servers.
- **Usage data**: anonymised screen and feature usage for analytics. This is
  off by default on iOS (App Tracking Transparency) and can be disabled at any
  time in Settings on both platforms.
- **Device identifiers**: a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push
  notifications, collected **only after** you opt in to notifications.
- **Support data**: if you contact support, the contents of your message and
  your account identifier so we can assist you.

We do **not** collect sensitive personal data, biometrics, government
identification numbers, or your contacts.

## 3. How we use it
To plan routes and costs; show places and stays near your route; sync your
saved trips, vehicles and expenses across devices; send notifications you
asked for; respond to support requests; and improve the App. All processing is
limited to what is necessary for the App to function and is proportionate to
that purpose.

## 4. What we do NOT do
We do **not** sell your personal data. We do **not** use it for advertising
without your consent. Route2Go may show partner stays and may earn an
affiliate commission when you book through a partner; that never changes the
price you pay on the partner's site. We do not perform profiling that produces
legal or similarly significant effects about you.

## 5. Sharing with partners
- **Firebase (Google)** — authentication and push notifications. Google's
  processing is governed by its own privacy policy.
- **Supabase** — database and serverless functions that store and process your
  trip data.
- **Map data providers** — your origin and destination coordinates are sent to
  a routing/geocoding provider (currently OpenStreetMap's Nominatim and OSRM)
  solely to compute a route and to geocode addresses. These are public map
  services; only the query coordinates are transmitted, never your identity.
- **Booking partners** — only when you choose to leave the App and book a
  stay; we log the booking click to track affiliate commission. Your booking
  is governed by the partner's own terms and privacy policy.

We require any processor to use your data only on our documented instructions
and only for the purposes described here.

## 6. How long we keep it
We keep your data for as long as your account is active and, after closure, only
for as long as required to comply with legal, tax and audit obligations (and in
any event not longer than is reasonably necessary). You can delete your account
at any time in Settings; deletion removes your trips, vehicles, expenses,
notifications, profile, and any associated records held for the functioning of
the App. Anonymised analytics that cannot be linked back to you may be retained
for product improvement.

## 7. Your rights
You have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **Correct** inaccurate data;
- **Export** your data in a portable format;
- **Delete** your data and your account;
- **Withdraw consent** at any time (for example, analytics or notifications);
- **Lodge a complaint** with the relevant data protection authority in India.

To exercise any of these rights, email **route2go1@gmail.com** and we will
respond within 30 days. Where the request is complex or numerous, we may extend
this period by up to a further 45 days and will tell you why.

## 8. Security
All traffic is over HTTPS. Your identity is verified with Firebase ID tokens
issued by Google, privileged operations run server-side using a restricted
service key that is never present in the App or in any committed code, and
database access is protected by row-level security so that you can only ever
see your own records. We follow reasonable administrative, technical and
physical safeguards appropriate to the risk.

## 9. Children
Route2Go is not intended for children under 18 and is not directed at children.
We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child
has provided us their data, contact **route2go1@gmail.com** and we will delete
it promptly.

## 10. Consent and withdrawing consent
By using the App you consent to the processing described in this policy to the
extent required by law. You may withdraw consent at any time by changing your
Settings or emailing us; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of
processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

## 11. Data transfer
Your data is stored on cloud infrastructure provided by Supabase and Firebase
and may be processed on servers located in India or in other jurisdictions.
Where data is transferred across borders, we rely on appropriate safeguards so
that your data is protected to a level substantially similar to Indian law.

## 12. Changes
We will post any changes to this policy at this page and, for material
changes, notify you in-app. Continued use of the App after a change is posted
constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review it
periodically.
