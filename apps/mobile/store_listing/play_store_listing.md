## Full Description
Route2Go is the road-trip planner that shows you the real cost before you go. Plan any trip across India (and beyond), compare route options by time and cost, and build a day-by-day itinerary — all in one app.

**What you get:**

• **Real route costs** — fuel and toll estimates calculated for your vehicle, not guesses. See the total trip cost upfront with a clear budget meter (green/yellow/red).

• **Compare your options** — see up to 5 route alternatives side by side (fastest, shortest, toll-free, recommended) with time and cost deltas so you can pick what works for you.

• **Places along your route** — discover cafes, restaurants, attractions, and rest stops near your path. See the exact detour cost and time before you add one.

• **Stays near your route** — find hotels and stays with honest pricing. Sponsored listings are clearly labelled.

• **Day-by-day itinerary** — auto-generate a realistic driving schedule with a configurable max-driving-hours safety cap. No more "drive 12 hours straight" plans.

• **Expense tracker** — log actual spending as you travel and compare against your budget. Split costs with your group.

• **Live trip navigation** — turn-by-turn directions with off-route deviation warnings, voice guidance, and text-to-speech.

• **Safety Centre** — manage trusted contacts, share your trip plan and ETA, and access emergency information.

• **Save & share trips** — keep your favourite routes, share trip details with friends, and pick up where you left off.

• **Privacy-first** — full account deletion, data export, and granular privacy controls. Your data stays yours.

**Built for Indian roads, works worldwide.**

Route2Go never fakes unavailable data — if a cost estimate isn't available, we tell you clearly instead of showing a made-up number.
