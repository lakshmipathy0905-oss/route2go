Your privacy matters

This Privacy Policy explains how Route2Go ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services. We are committed to transparency and to giving you control over your data.

1. Who we are

Route2Go is operated by Together (Lakshmipathy and Team), with its operational contact address at JP Nagar, Kothanuru Dinne, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560076, India. Route2Go is operated as a personal, non-company venture. Where this policy says "we", "us" or "our", it means the operator identified above.

This policy applies to everyone who uses the App, including guests who use it without an account.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, display name (if you provide one), and the Firebase user ID issued when you create an account (via Google Sign-In or email/password).
  • Trip planning data: origin and destination, route options, vehicle details (fuel type and mileage), budget and itinerary choices, and your saved trips.
  • Location: precise location is used only when you actively choose a route start/end point, or begin a live trip (for on-route deviation checks). We do not collect background location outside an active live trip, and we never upload a continuous GPS track to our servers.
  • Usage data: anonymised screen and feature usage for analytics. This is off by default on iOS (App Tracking Transparency) and can be disabled at any time in Settings on both platforms.
  • Device identifiers: a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push notifications, collected only after you opt in to notifications.
  • Support data: if you contact support, the contents of your message and your account identifier so we can assist you.

We do not collect sensitive personal data, biometrics, government identification numbers, or your contacts.

3. How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • Plan routes and estimate fuel/toll costs
  • Show places and stays near your route
  • Sync your saved trips, vehicles, and expenses across devices
  • Send notifications you asked for
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve the App

All processing is limited to what is necessary for the App to function and is proportionate to that purpose.

4. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use it for advertising without your consent.
  • Route2Go may show partner stays and may earn an affiliate commission when you book through a partner; that never changes the price you pay on the partner's site.
  • We do not perform profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you.

5. Sharing with partners

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and push notifications. Google's processing is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Supabase — database and serverless functions that store and process your trip data.
  • Map data providers — your origin and destination coordinates are sent to a routing/geocoding provider (currently OpenStreetMap's Nominatim and Valhalla) solely to compute a route and to geocode addresses. These are public map services; only the query coordinates are transmitted, never your identity.
  • Booking partners — only when you choose to leave the App and book a stay; we log the booking click to track affiliate commission. Your booking is governed by the partner's own terms and privacy policy.

We require any processor to use your data only on our documented instructions and only for the purposes described here.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active and, after closure, only for as long as necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When you delete your account, we permanently remove your trips, vehicles, and personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (see our account deletion page)
  • Withdraw consent for analytics at any time in Settings
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

8. Children's privacy

Route2Go is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS on all API calls)
  • Row Level Security (RLS) on our Supabase database so users can only access their own data
  • Firebase Authentication for secure identity management
  • Regular security reviews of our code and infrastructure

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy in the App and on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
